## Runtime data, not compiled software

- **PC BIOS 8x8 font** (`font.c`): the 256-glyph ROM font at F000:FA6E
  (linear 0x000FFA6E) is copied from the BIOS ROM at boot on machines that
  have it. The font ROM bytes are part of the IBM PC compatibility
  convention; a checksum-validated embedded fallback atlas is used when the
  copy fails.
- **Multiboot / Multiboot2** information-structure layouts handled by the
  boot stub (`src/boot/boot.s`) are documented in the public Multiboot
  specification: https://www.gnu.org/software/grub/manual/multiboot2/
